# PoliSense
***

## Phase 1
Phase-1 deals with implementing filtering & reporting mechanisms, of relevant policies applicable to a given company based on the company metadata   

### Goals
- Implementing an effective embedding model to represent knowledge documents on Grants,Schemes & other policies
- Identifying metadata for querying & indexing the embedding model
- Implementing retrieval mechanisms
- Validating retrieved information relevancy
- Implementing ranking module to rank retreived information on policies. Ranking is to be done on decreasing order of __*VALUE*__ the policies generate. The term __*VALUE*__ is yet to be defined
- Validating ranked outcomes
